Springer Nature

Springer Nature's journals cover sciences, technology, medicine, and social sciences. Their portfolio includes Intensive Care Medicine, Journal of Epidemiology and Global Health, and Anatomical Science International.

Elsevier

Elsevier focuses on scientific journals and publishes almost 3,000 different titles. Medical journals include Clinical Oncology, The Journal of Emergency Medicine, and American Journal of Obstetrics & Gynecology.

Sage

Sage publishes over 1,100 journals, including: Qualitative Health Research, Ear, Nose, & Throat Journal, Women's Health Reports, and more.

Wiley

Wiley publishes journals across arts, humanities, social sciences, and hard sciences. Their medical journals include Hepatology, Clinical Pharmacology and Therapeautics, and Medicinal Research Reviews.
