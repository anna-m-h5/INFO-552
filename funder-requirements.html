Funder Requirements

NIH

Publishing

The NIH recently underwent a shift in publishing policy. As of July 2025, all articles accepted for publication must be submitted to PubMed Central, to be available immediately without an embargo. You must ensure that any journal contracts you sign do not conflict with this policy.

NIH Public Access Policy

DMPs

As of May 25, 2026, all DMPs must be in the 2026 Pilot DMS Plan format. Technically, the data mangement and sharing policy has not changed, the updated format is mostly multiple choice and requires only one narrative answer.

2026 DMS Plan Format NIH Data Management and Sharing Policy

NSF

DMPs

NSF's data management and sharing plan requirements are currently in flux and have been causing confusing among researchers. As of April 2026, NSF no longer requires PDF versions of DMPs. Instead, investigators must complete a survey directly in Research.gov. While the proposed webform is seemingly available here, its functionality is limited. Librarians and research offices across the country are following these changes as they occur - reach out to Drexel's Office of Research & Innovations with questions about your proposal.
