Managing Sensitive Data

Deidentifying Data

Research protocols must be approved by Drexel's Institutional Review Board (IRB); the Human Research Protection Program (HRPP) provides guidance to the IRB on research involving human subjects. Personally identifying data must be deidentifed before sharing, and access to this data throughout the study should be carefully controlled with varying levels of permissions.

Johns Hopkins University has a thorough Protecting Human Subject Identifiers LibGuide that covers specific steps for de-identifying data, advanced techniques for anonymization, and more.

Protecting Human Subject Identifiers by JHU

REDCap

REDCap, or Research Electronic Data Capture, is a secure web application for collecting and managing data. It is HIPAA-compliant and allows for highly customizable user permissions and data access groups.
