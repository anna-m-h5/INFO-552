Professional Associations

Research Data Access & Preservation Association | 2026 - Present

  • The Research Data Access & Preservation Association (RDAP) is a community dedicated to research data management. Members occupy a wide range of positions: librarians, archivists, data scientists, developers, and more.

American Library Association | 2026 - Present

  • The American Library Association (ALA) houses the Association for College and Research Libraries, the Reference and User Services Association, and other library groups in the United States. ALA creates standards for library service and accredits Master of Library and Information Science programs.

Pennsylvania Library Association | 2026 - Present

  • The Pennsylvania Library Association (PaLA) "strengthens Pennsylvania's libraries through advocacy, education, and leadership."

Medical Library Association Liberty Chapter | 2025 - Present

  • The Medical Library Association (MLA) connects health science information professionals and offers comprehensive continuing education certificates and materials.
  • The MLA Liberty Chapter is the MLA chapter serving the Philadelphia region of Pennsylvania, New Jersey, Delaware, and New York.
