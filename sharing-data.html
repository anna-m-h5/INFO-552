Repositories

National Library of Medicine List

The National Library of Medicine offers a list of repositories that may meet data sharing requirements. On this page, you can search either generalist or domain-specific repositories, and can sort by data access properties to find a repository that meets your project's needs. Most repositories for human data do not support open data submission and require some information about the investigators and project before accepting data.

Registry of Research Data Repositories

re3data is a database of possible repositories to deposit your research data. You can browse by country and hierarchical subjects and filter by data access restrictions, PID systems, content types, and more.

Dryad

Drexel is not an institutional member of Dryad, so depositing data comes with a fee. If you intend to deposit data in Dryad, you can build this fee into your grant proposal and research costs.

Deposit with Dryad

QDR

Qualitative data may meet the threshold of NIH's data sharing requirements. The Qualitative Data Repository, or QDR, is a generalist repository supported by Syracuse University.
