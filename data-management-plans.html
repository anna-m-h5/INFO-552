Data Management Plans (DMPs)

What is a data management plan?

Data Management Plans, sometimes called Data Management and Sharing Plans, are documents created before the start of a research project that describe how the data from the research will be stored and managed. Generally, DMPs should address:

  • Description
    • What standards will be used?
    • How will these standards be applied to the data?
    • How do these standards ensure interoperability and data reuse?
  • Files
    • What file format(s) will be used for each type of data?
    • Which app(s) will store the data during and after the project?
  • Sharing
    • Which data will be made available and why?
    • Where will the data be deposited?
    • Will access to specific data be controlled?
  • Privacy
    • How will data be deidentified?
    • How will confidentiality be ensured?
  • Oversight
    • Who at the project's institution will be monitoring data management?
    • How frequently will data management be assessed?
